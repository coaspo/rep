Mindfulness Exercises:
  1. Body Scan Meditation:
    How to do it: Find a quiet and comfortable place to sit or lie down. Close your
    eyes and take a few deep breaths. Start mentally scanning your body from the top
    of your head down to your toes. Notice any sensations, tension, or relaxation in
    each part of your body. Don't try to change anything; just observe.
    When to practice: Ideally, do this meditation daily, especially after your morning
    walk or before bed. It can be as short as 5-10 minutes.

  2. Mindful Breathing:
    How to do it: Sit comfortably with your back straight or lie down. Close your eyes
    and focus on your breath. Notice the sensation of air entering and leaving your
    nostrils. If your mind wanders, gently bring your attention back to your breath.
    When to practice: This can be done in short sessions throughout the day to quickly
    center yourself, especially during high-stress moments.

  3. Progressive Muscle Relaxation:
    How to do it: Begin by tensing and then relaxing different muscle groups in your
    body. Start with your toes, tense them as tightly as you can for a few seconds,
    then relax. Move up through your body—calves, thighs, stomach, chest, arms, hands,
    neck, face—tensing and relaxing each group in turn.
    When to practice: This is effective right before bed or anytime you feel
    particularly stressed during the day.

Additional Stretches:
  1. Downward-Facing Dog (Adho Mukha Svanasana):
    How to do it: From a tabletop position on your hands and knees, tuck your toes
    under and lift your hips up and back, straightening your arms and legs. Keep your
    body in an inverted V shape. Hold for 30 seconds to a minute.
    Benefits: Stretches the hamstrings, calves, arches, and hands while strengthening
    the arms and legs.

  2. Pigeon Pose (Eka Pada Rajakapotasana):
    How to do it: Start on all fours, bring your right knee forward and place it
    behind your right wrist, extending your left leg behind you. Allow your torso
    to fold over your front thigh. Hold for 30 seconds to a minute, then switch sides.
    Benefits: Deeply stretches hips, glutes, and piriformis muscles.

  3. Butterfly Stretch (Baddha Konasana):
    How to do it: Sit on the floor with the soles of your feet together, let your
    knees fall out to the sides. Use your elbows to gently press your knees down
    toward the floor. Hold for 30 seconds to a minute.
    Benefits: Great for opening the hips and groin area.

  4. Seated Forward Bend (Paschimottanasana):
    How to do it: Sit on the floor with your legs extended in front of you. Inhale
    and lengthen your spine, then exhale and hinge at the hips, reaching for your
    feet, shins, or blocks. Hold for 30 seconds to a minute.
    Benefits: Stretches the hamstrings and lower back.
    Remember, it's important to stretch gently and never force a stretch. Listen to
    your body, respect its limits, and breathe deeply throughout. These exercises
    should be added to your existing routine gradually.
