The next of kin certificate is typically issued by the municipality in Greece, where the deceased had his family record. If the family record is not appropriately updated,(i.e. marriage and/or birth of children is not registered in Greece), then there are some alternative procedures, which you could discuss with your attorney in Greece.

1) You can update your family record, which means that you would have to register any acts of births, marriages etc .

2) The second alternative procedure is as follows: Two Greek Citizens that knew the deceased well enough can sign an affidavit (υπεύθυνη δήλωση) attesting that they knew the deceased very well and that they can testify for his/her next of kin, at the time of his/her death. Those two GREEK CITIZENS can either come to the Consulate to sign before an officer the above mentioned affidavits with the ratification of their signatures, or if they reside in Greece, they can present themselves before any Police Department or any KEP (Κέντρο Εξυπηρέτησης Πολιτών) in order to sign the affidavit. These affidavits are presented before the competent Municipality in order for the Next of Kin Document to be issued.

 3) Thirdly, you can issue a next of kin certificate from the Probate Court in the US, (https://www.mass.gov/doc/instructions-for-form-mpc-162-surviving-spouse-children-heirs-at-law-mpc-958/download) place the APOSTILLE stamp and have it translated into Greek.
