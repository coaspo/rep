Intoduction
When they one said the last goodbye, everyone was crying.  It was surreal for
to see adults cry. When we got up next day before daybreak it was cold and
damp because it had been raining all night long. The whole family family, two
adults and three kids 11, 8, 1 1/2 years old, boarded the bus to go to America.
I was the 11 year old kid.

I learned later that my grandmother tried to stop us from leaving by promising
to give us the land that my father had asked for.  His caustic reply to his
mother in law was "you can hug the rocks".  It was a bad omen for things to come.

The bus started winding down the mountain on a dirt road. Near the bottom of
the mountain the bus stopped and everyone got out so that the bus could navigate
the mud was blocking the road.  The tears the night before had turned to rain
and in vain tried to prevent them leaving.
