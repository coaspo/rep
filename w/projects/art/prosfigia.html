    Like a celebratory joke. The refugee.

Look at my hands
in the deep weird ravines
where the centuries of loneliness
rolled through to bring us sometime together

Look in my eyes
the fading farmland with the patrimonial hut
in the byway to the bamboo field
up to the shadow of the boatman
who guards the grass of childhood years
in the ford of Eurotas

look at the fever marks on the skin
and the wild-beasts of the desert
in the thirsty ancestral vineyard
see the little bites of you being the home longing outcast refugee in a foreign land
on my funny belly
that folds time
in scale's trays
the mole on the neck that it touched
that always bled since childhood
in the rambling of the game
notice the reminiscing of the blood tests
with the fragile sugar curve

I have thought up new tales to tell you
so come out for a while
on the narrow balcony with its freshly painted railings
and the empty nests of the sparrows
that first chased away the swallows
but they too later kicked out
who knows why this invisible war
here in the side light of February

come grab my cold nose
listen to life as it wanes
in my nostrils
and cut from my breast
the silence of the parting
then copy in your mouth
my shy words
that have fallen asleep waiting
on my parched tongue

and then in one of your bites drink my wine
leave me there in the embrace to bask with you
like the fluffy partridge
on top the rock of the day
and don't speak don't say
who is really
celebrating.16
