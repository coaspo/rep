  Different types of AI
    mimic, enhance, or exceed human capabilities.
    1. Diagnostic/descriptive AI: patterns/trends
       assessing the correctness of behavior
         Scenario planning: scenarios based on historical data.
         Pattern/trends recognition: patterns/trends
         Comparative analysis: correlations and insights.
         Root cause analysis: find underlying reasons behind specific outcomes.
    2. Predictive AI
       customer behavior, market trends
         Forecasting: Predicts future trends and events.
         Clustering and classification: Group/classify into predefined categories.
         Propensity model: likelihood of specific outcomes
         Decision trees: tree-like model of decisions to predict outcomes.
    3. Prescriptive AI
       optimal course of action
         Personalization: recommendations/experiences to individual needs.
         Optimization: most efficient ways to achieve goals.
         Fraud prevention: Detects/prevents fraud
         Next best action recommendation: next steps to take.
    4. Generative/cognitive AI
        mimic human creativity to automate and assist in content creation.
          Advises: expert advice/recommendations.
          Creates: Produces text, images, and code.
          Protects: security measures t
          Assists: improve efficiency.
          Automates: Automates repetitive tasks to save time/resources.
    5. Reactive AI
       respond to specific inputs with predetermined responses - no memory
         Rule-based actions: act on predefined rules.
         Instant responses: immediate responses to inputs.
         Static data analysis: Analyzes current data without past interactions.
    6. Limited memory AI:
       learn from historical data; autonomous vehicles and recommendation systems
         Learning from data: history to make informed decisions.
         Pattern recognition: over time to improve accuracy.
         Adaptive responses: based on previous interactions.
    7. Theory of Mind AI:
       understand human emotions, beliefs, and intentions (research)
       interact more naturally with humans by comprehending their mental states
          Emotion recognition: Identify/respond to human emotions.
          Social interaction: natural and human-like interactions.
          Intent prediction: Predicts human intentions based on context and behavior.
    8. Self-aware AI:
       most advanced AI, has consciousness/self-awareness
       can understand and react to its own emotions (theoretical concept)
          Self-diagnosis: Evaluates its own performance and health.
          Autonomous learning: Learns independently without human intervention.
          Adaptive behavior: Adjusts behavior based on self-awareness.
    9. Narrow AI (Weak AI):
       excels in a single area but lacks generalization capabilities
       (Most current AI applications)
          Task specialization: Excel in specific tasks.
          High accuracy: high performance in its designated area.
          Efficiency: within its scope of specialization.
    10. General AI (Strong AI):
        understand, learn, and apply knowledge for wide range of tasks
        transfer knowledge between domains and adapt autonomously.
          Cross-domain learning:
          Autonomous decision making: in diverse scenarios.
          Human-like understanding:

  Evolution of AI: Traditional AI vs. Generative AI
     Traditional AI
       1. repository
       2. analytics platform
          example SPSS modeler (data mining and text analytics)
             or Watson Studio (optimize decisions)
       3. application layer
          feedback loop to 1. (learn from previous mistakes/successes)
     Generative AI
       1. Data of everything (massive)
       2. LLM (large lang. models)
       3. Prompting & tuning - specific to organization
       4. Application
          feedback loop to 3.
          (Data and models are huge)
