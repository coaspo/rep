    Factors for crisis resolution
      1. Acknowledgment of crisis - seek help
      2. Acceptance of personal responsibility
         no blaming, or self pity
         others involved but I cannot control them
      3. Build a fence
         not totally flawed - what is and is not Ok
         selective changer is key
      4. Help from others
         from talking, to finding new job
      5. Other people as models
         friend, acquaintance, or biography of celebrity
      6. Ego strength
         self confidence, sense of purpose, self acceptance, proudly independent
         tolerate strong emotions, keep focused under stress,
         express yourself freely, perceive reality accurately, make sound decisions
         (comes from parents)
      7. Honest self approval
         precondition for making sound decisions
         people are usually not honest - may overestimate and underestimate
      8. Experience of previous crisis
         if you did not master previous crisis, you will be helpless
      9. Patience
         tolerate uncertainty and failure, keep on trying
     10. Flexibility
         there is more than one way - being a parent
         inflexibility caused by abuse or trauma
     11. core values
         moral code, family values
         may help or hinder crisis resolution
     12. Freedom from constraints
         family, job, physical danger
    Coping depends on culture - culture effects national crisis resolution
    overlap of personal & national characteristics
